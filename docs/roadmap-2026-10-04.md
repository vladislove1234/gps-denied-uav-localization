# Roadmap: where to go next (2026-10-04)

Written after a full review of experiments 001–021, the 021 correction records,
`builds/corrector-identity-bench`, the gate ROC follow-up, the Pi 5 timings and
the PoC notes. Numbers are from `runs.jsonl`, `artifacts/*.csv` and the build
notes; nothing here is recalled from memory.

## Where we stand

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Best pipeline: segmenter + weighted NCC, 5.0–5.9 m @10 m injected (sim cross-date, 40 frames) | `007.1`, 021 `correction-records-*.csv` |
| Corrector geometry is clean: 0.17 m floor on a self-match | `builds/corrector-identity-bench` |
| Window reach caps everything: truth reachable 56 % @10 m, 10 % @15 m | identity bench, 005 |
| 8 of 40 frames are blind (no weighted class), 20 % of draws stuck at the prior | identity bench |
| Error lives on bushcha (7–8 m), not birky (3 m). Worst cases: look-alikes along a road, hallucinated track, flat woody | 018, 021 `extreme_cases` |
| Perfect masks buy ~1 m. Edges, SegFix, CRF, boundary and building losses: 0 m on position | 006, 018, 021 |
| Gate cuts harm 3× at +1 m mean error and never touches p90 or max. Per-frame confidence statistics do not transfer between places (LOPO AUC 0.597 < s1 0.717) | 007, `gate-roc.ipynb` |
| Real-flight pose error (heading ±25° per pass, footprint scale 0.6) is worth 2.5–4 m @5 m | 004, `pocs/pose-reregistration` |
| Drone-domain gap unmeasured since 005: all later work is satellite-as-drone | 005–021 |
| Training data: 2 hand-labelled villages. Val plateaus by step 500–1200, train–val gap 0.25 | 021 |
| Pi 5: matching is 85–90 % of a correction, ~120 ms per class, direct `conv2d` | `pocs/seg-correction-pi5` |

Conclusion: the segmentation side is exhausted on this bench. The levers left
are the benchmark itself, the matching function, temporal fusion, training
data, and the real drone domain.

## Ideas, ranked

### Tier 1. Cheap, unblocks everything

1. **Widen the test ground.** Rebuild `village-crossdate-frames` over the ~57
   villages the segmenters never saw. A few hundred non-overlapping frames
   instead of 40 (20 of which overlap on one 270 m strip). Every CI today
   carries the "2 places, too narrow" caveat. Also enables leave-one-place-out
   tuning, which is what falsified both gate ideas.
2. **Fix window reach.** Report reachable-only and all-draws separately, or set
   the window from the prior uncertainty (INS covariance × k) instead of a
   fixed 1.7× footprint. The 15 m numbers currently measure window size, not
   matching.
3. **FFT / integral-image NCC.** Lewis-style fast normalised cross-correlation
   with running sums. Cuts ~120 ms per class to a few ms. Pure engineering,
   verified by the identity bench (must reproduce the 0.17 m floor). Makes
   ideas 4 and 6 affordable on the Pi.

### Tier 2. Matching and temporal fusion

4. **Multi-hypothesis tracking** (chosen direction, 2026-10-03). Top-K heatmap
   peaks after non-max suppression, each hypothesis carried by the INS motion,
   score accumulated over fixes, laggards pruned, neighbours merged. Put a yaw
   offset in the hypothesis state: covers real-flight heading bias for free.
   Evaluate on the flight simulator over the 47 test villages: end-point error
   and divergence share vs the replace policy and INS-only (008's benchmark
   design without the particle filter).
5. **Distance-transform (chamfer) likelihood for track.** Score drone track
   pixels by distance to the nearest map track pixel instead of pixel overlap.
   Tolerates width mismatch (OSM 2–3× too wide, 2018 vs 2025 road width: the
   birky-15 slide) and small misalignment. SWA-PF uses this. Same 006-style
   ablation, cheap.
6. **Heading + scale search** (003, never run). No gain on sim frames (pose is
   exact), mandatory for real flights. With FFT NCC the ~35 evaluations per
   draw are cheap. Fold into the MHT yaw state rather than a standalone run.
7. **Multi-date map aggregation.** Match against every stored Wayback date and
   sum the heatmaps. Or build a "persistent structure" map: a pixel is building
   only if it is building on every date. Kills date-specific look-alikes. Data
   already on disk, config-only change.
8. **Class-weight sweep, leave-one-place-out.** Weights 0/1/1/0.5/0/0.5 were
   fixed in the 002 proposal and never tuned. Also test field > 0: parcel edges
   are structure and would unblind some of the 20 % field frames.
9. **Gate pass-through band.** Only if a gate is kept at all: accept when
   peak_z is high regardless of s1 (s1 measures window ambiguity and has a 35 %
   false-alarm rate on correct matches). MHT makes the gate obsolete.
10. **Building-centroid matching** (020). Run the oracle arm first. Only if 5
    and 7 plateau.

### Tier 3. Model and training

11. **Cross-date consistency training.** Same ground, different date, must give
    the same mask. Free supervision from 59 villages × 2–5 dates. Trains
    "survive the domain gap" directly. Pretrain on OSM + MS weak labels over
    all 59 villages (offset-corrected), fine-tune on the hand labels. Targets
    the real bottleneck: 2 villages of data.
12. **Georeferenced pseudo-labels for drone frames.** Warp the satellite class
    map into each drone frame via the logged pose (the "addressable by
    coordinate" rule). The 4,137 archive pairs become drone training data.
    Pose errors add noise; reposed poses from `pocs/pose-reregistration` reduce
    it. Then per-domain BN. The 021 models never saw a drone frame.
13. **Learned correlation head.** Small CNN over (drone probability map,
    satellite probability patch) → heatmap, trained with cross-entropy over the
    true offset. Training pairs are free from multidate crops with a known
    shift. Learns which classes transfer across dates instead of hand weights.
    Risk: overfitting to few villages; train on 57, validate LOPO.
14. **Localization-aware fine-tune.** Backprop a position loss through the
    differentiable NCC into the segmenter, so it becomes a matchability
    extractor with a semantic initialisation. A genuine thesis contribution.
    Do after 13 works.
15. **Architecture: stop here.** Stride 8 costs 0.4 m. U-Net R18 ≈ LR-ASPP on
    position error and 4× slower on the Pi. Keep LR-ASPP. SegFormer-B0 is worth
    one probe (cross-domain claims) after 11.
16. **Model ensemble.** Average the R18 + R34 + LR-ASPP probability maps.
    Cheap, checkpoints exist, expect a small gain.

### Tier 4. Proof on real data

17. **Drone-side ceiling on real frames** (006 Next #2, never run).
    `ua-drone-annotated` as the GT query on the 2025 frames. Answers whether
    drone segmentation is the bottleneck in the field.
18. **Real-flight run.** The Gonchary 2025 sequence through the
    flight-simulator loop with real frames: 021 model + drone BN + MHT +
    heading search. The end-to-end proof for the thesis.

## Recommended order

1 → 3 → 4 (with 6 inside) → 5, 7, 8 as ablations on the widened bench →
11, 12 → 18. Ideas 13/14 if time allows: the strongest novelty claim.

**Skip:** more mask refiners, more loss terms, per-frame gates, the particle
filter.
