# drone-2025-easy — dataset card

> Equipment. Annotator **input**, not labels. `manifest.json` is generated by
> `build.py`.

## What this is

The 44 drone frames from the 2025 flight that were hand-picked as "easy"
(frames with roads) and used for the validation runs of the 2025 work. One
folder per frame: `frame.jpg` (1920×1080) and `meta.json`.

## Source

| Sample = | one frame; labels are drawn in **frame pixels** (no georeference) |

## How it was built

`build.py` takes the timestamps of the two "easy" folders and copies each frame
from its complete parent folder: 11 of the files in the "easy" folders are 0
bytes. Position, altitude and yaw are parsed from the filenames, which the 2025
pipeline wrote from the MAVLink log.

## Known problems

- `mpp` is a nadir approximation (63° horizontal FOV at the logged altitude,
  ~3–4 cm/px); camera tilt and terrain are ignored. Treat it as ±20 %. It only
  sizes brushes and road widths in metres; labels do not depend on it.
- 44 frames, one flight, one season, one area. Consecutive frames 1 s apart
  overlap: split by time block, never randomly.
