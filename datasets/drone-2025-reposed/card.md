# drone-2025-reposed — dataset card

> Equipment, not an experiment. There is no bar to miss here — this either
> exists and is sound, or it does not. Written by hand; `manifest.json`
> alongside it is generated by `build.ipynb` and holds the numbers.

## What this is

The 44 `drone-2025-easy` frames with a corrected pose. Heading and footprint
scale are replaced per frame by the values `pocs/pose-reregistration` fitted.
The logged position is kept. One folder per frame:

- `frame.jpg`: the drone frame, unchanged.
- `satellite.png`: the 2025 orthophoto, cropped heading-up at the logged
  position, corrected heading and corrected footprint, at the orthophoto's own
  0.199 m/px.
- `label.png`: the hand labels (`ua-ortho-annotated/gonchary-2025`, class ids,
  0 = unlabelled) on exactly the same grid, nearest-neighbour.
- `meta.json`: `load_frames()`-compatible. `yaw_deg` is the corrected heading,
  `alt_m` is the logged altitude × footprint scale (an *effective* altitude:
  the footprint is linear in it), and `mpp` is scaled with it. The logged values
  are kept as `logged_yaw_deg` / `logged_alt_m`, together with `yaw_offset_deg`,
  `footprint_scale`, `block` and `ambiguity_m`.

`load_frames("drone-2025-reposed")` works as a drop-in for `drone-2025-easy`.

## How it was built

`build.ipynb`: for each frame, it reads the PoC's `corrected_yaw_deg` and
`scale`, rewrites `meta.json` and crops both rasters with the correlator's
footprint (FOV 60°, 16:9) at the new pose.

|---|---|---|
| frames | 44 | 44 |
| position changed vs `drone-2025-easy` | 0 | 0.0° |
| satellite and label crop shapes equal | all | yes |

## Known problems

- **The corrections were fitted against the same hand map these labels come
  from, using weighted class NCC.** Any experiment that scores class-map
  matching on this map is scored at the pose its own objective prefers.
  Circular for evaluation (see `experiments/004-reposed-frames/proposal.md`).
- **Position is the logged one.** The PoC's position fix slides a median 36 m
  along the track, so it was left out. The logged position has its own error,
  unmeasured.
- **Scale is weakly determined.** The score against scale is flat, and 7/44
  frames sit at the search floor, 0.5. Treat `alt_m` as ±20%.
- **Heading is determined by one linear feature** (the track). Where the frame
  has only the track, a mirrored or slightly wrong heading could score similarly.
  Not checked beyond the preview.
- The labels cover track and woody only (building has no labels here).

## What this is NOT valid for

- **Benchmark truth for class-map matching on the Gonchary hand map.** It is
  circular (see above).
- **Scale.** 44 frames of one flight. The ~4,000 frames of the 2025
  `image_pairs_v2` set are **not** reposed. Their passes would need their own
  corrections, estimated on frames not used for evaluation.
- **Position ground truth.**
