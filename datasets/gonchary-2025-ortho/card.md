# gonchary-2025-ortho — dataset card

> Equipment. Annotator **input**. `manifest.json` is generated by `build.py`.

The raw 2025 Gonchary Google Earth frame
(`archive/uav_localization_2025/data/satelites/satellite_gonchary.jpg`,
8192×6026, ~6.7 cm/px, the image experiment 001's harness reads), downsampled
3× (LANCZOS) to 2731×2009, ~0.2 m/px, so the annotator stays within browser
memory. One location, `gonchary-2025`, one image `ortho.jpg`, pixel-only.

Not the `_corrected` / `_cutted` exports: those are warped into the flight box
and mask the village out (50% black).
