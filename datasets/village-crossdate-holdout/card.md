# village-crossdate-holdout — dataset card

> Equipment, not an experiment. `manifest.json` alongside it is generated by
> `village-crossdate-frames/build.ipynb` (run through papermill with this name)
> and holds the numbers.

## What this is

The same simulated drone frames as `village-crossdate-frames`, cut the same way,
on 10 other villages. Each frame is the newest Esri date (`current`) cropped
heading-up at a random position and heading, footprint 46 × 26 m (60° camera at
40 m). It is the second test bench of `023-learned-correlation-head`: a check
that a matcher's result on the two `village-crossdate-frames` places is not
specific to them.

## Source

| | |
|---|---|
| Derived from | `ua-village-multidate` |
| Site / coverage | the 10 val villages of 023's location split (`village_split`, seed 42): ivannie, maidan, maksymivka, oblaznytsia, pidhaichyky, pidhorodnie, sadzhavka, strilche, vesnianka, zlynets |
| Capture date(s) | drone view: `current`; `meta.json` names the oldest date, the bench matches against the newest |
| Sample = | one crop + its pose |
| Split | all test. No segmenter trained here; 023's heads only watch these villages as val, they never select on them |

Built by `papermill build.ipynb ... -f holdout-params.yaml` with `NAME`,
`PLACES` (10 villages, whole tile) and `DESCRIPTION` changed; every other value is
the frames notebook's default (20 frames per place, 40 m, 80 m margin, seed 42).
Executed copy: `experiments/023-learned-correlation-head/artifacts/build-holdout.ipynb`.

## Acceptance checks

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| frames | 200 (20 per place) |
| drone view and map agree at the same pose | visually, one frame per place in `preview.png` |

## Known problems

- Not drone imagery: coarse, perfectly nadir, no perspective.
- Most villages hold one Wayback date, so the map gap is whatever that date is
  (2023-03 to 2025-04 against `current`).
- Random positions: some frames hold no building, track, woody or water at all.

## What this is NOT valid for

Anything about the drone-to-satellite gap, drone resolution, or altitude effects.
